Privacy Policy

How Cambric Utopia collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Cambric Utopia (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes www.cambricutopia.com, an independent fashion guide. We care about your privacy and collect only what we need. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

What you tell us

If you write to us using our contact form, we receive your name, email address, topic and message. If you join The Utopia Letter, we receive your email address. We use this information solely to reply to you or to send the newsletter. We do not sell clothing and never request payment details.

What we collect automatically

We automatically receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and the time of your visit. The capsule checklist on our homepage saves your ticks only in your own browser’s storage; we never receive them. Cookies are described in our Cookie Policy.

How we use it

  • To respond to messages and corrections.
  • To send the monthly newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides readers find useful.
  • To protect the website from spam and misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic. It uses cookies to collect information such as the pages you view and how long you stay, processed by Google under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display ads from Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help run the website, such as hosting and email delivery, or when the law requires it.

Retention

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data follows the retention settings in our Google Analytics account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No online system is entirely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally; the date above shows the current version.
