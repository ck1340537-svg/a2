Editorial Policy

The standards behind every guide on Cambric Utopia.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

We want readers to trust what they read on Cambric Utopia. These are the standards we follow for every guide.

Independence

We do not accept payment, gifted clothing or affiliate commissions in exchange for coverage, and we keep our guides brand-neutral. If advertising appears on the website, advertisers have no influence over our content.

Research

Our fabric explanations draw on textile references and glossaries, conversations with makers and tailors, and hands-on experience wearing and caring for fine cottons. Where terms are used inconsistently in the industry, we say so.

Writing standards

  • We explain technical terms clearly and avoid unnecessary jargon.
  • We describe styling ideas as suggestions, not rules.
  • We do not make exaggerated claims about sustainability or quality.
  • We encourage caring for, repairing and reusing clothing.

Corrections

If you notice an error, please contact us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Fabric swatches and diagrams are illustrations created by us.
