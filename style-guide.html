02Wear it well

Styling fine cotton for every day

Practical ideas for wearing shirts, blouses and light cottons through the year, from the office to the weekend and into the evening.

AEveryday

Five ways to wear a white shirt

  1. Half-tucked into straight jeans with loafers for an effortless weekend look.
  2. Fully tucked into tailored trousers with a slim belt for the office.
  3. Open over a vest as a light summer layer with shorts or a skirt.
  4. Under a fine knit with just the collar and cuffs showing in cooler months.
  5. Knotted at the waist over a slip skirt or wide trousers on warm evenings.

CColour

Beyond white

White is the classic, but fine cottons look beautiful in soft colour too. Pale shirting blue is perhaps the most versatile: it pairs with navy, grey, khaki, denim and black, and flatters a wide range of complexions.

Fine stripes add interest without being loud. Look for narrow stripes in blue, grey or soft red on a white ground; they are just as easy to wear as plain shirts.

For prints, smaller, lighter-weight motifs on lawn or voile suit the delicacy of the fabric better than large, heavy designs.

DAt home

Cotton for rest

Historically, cambric and batiste were prized for nightwear because they are soft, breathable and absorbent. That still makes sense today.

  • Choose a relaxed fit with room to move; piped edges add a classic finish.
  • Lightweight cotton keeps you comfortable on warm nights and layers under a robe when it’s cool.
  • Wash sleepwear often on a gentle cycle; fine cotton softens beautifully with each wash.
